🛡️ Cybersecurity

Learn the fundamentals of cybersecurity, digital security, online safety and protection of computer systems and networks.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks and other security threats.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

Today, computers and the internet are used for communication, education, banking, business and many other activities. Cybersecurity helps protect important systems and information.

Ethical & Legal Practice

Cybersecurity skills should be practiced only on systems that you own or have explicit permission to test. Learning through authorized labs and training environments helps develop security skills safely and responsibly.
