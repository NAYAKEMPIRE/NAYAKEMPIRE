🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Explore the world of Artificial Intelligence and learn how machines can process information, recognize patterns and perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks such as understanding language, recognizing images, analyzing information and making predictions.

AI + Cybersecurity: Artificial Intelligence can also be used to help detect unusual activity, analyze security data and support cybersecurity professionals.

Important AI Areas

🧠 Machine Learning

Systems learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

💬 Natural Language Processing

Technology that allows computers to process and understand human language.

👁️ Computer Vision

AI systems can analyze images and videos to identify objects, patterns and information.

✨ Generative AI

AI models can generate new text, images, audio, code and other types of content.
