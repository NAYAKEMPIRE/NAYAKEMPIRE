🌐 Networking

Learn how computers, phones, servers and other devices communicate with each other through networks.

What is Networking?

Computer networking is the process of connecting devices so they can communicate and share information. Networks can connect computers, smartphones, servers, printers and many other devices.

Important Networking Concepts

📍 IP Address

An IP address identifies a device on a network and helps data reach the correct destination.

🔌 Ports

Ports help network services communicate with applications on a device.

🌍 DNS

DNS translates human-readable domain names into IP addresses.

📡 Router

A router forwards network traffic between different networks.

Networking Fundamentals

Why Networking Matters in Cybersecurity

Networking is one of the most important foundations of cybersecurity. Understanding how data travels across networks makes it easier to understand security threats, monitoring, firewalls and secure communication.
